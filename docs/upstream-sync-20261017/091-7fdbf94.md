### Lifecycle and errors

- Prepare enables the five regulator supplies, toggles reset, then runs init. The initial zero-brightness write remains in the multi-command context: a negative DCS transfer error becomes `accum_err`, suppresses subsequent multi commands, and is returned. The existing prepare error path attempts bulk regulator disable and returns the original init error.
- On 7.2, the enable callback sends display-on and waits 20 ms. A callback error prevents generic backlight enable. On both pinned kernels, the generic helper enables the backlight after the optional panel callback; it restores the existing desired level through the driver. See [Linux 7.2.8 DRM panel core](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gregkh/linux/v7.2.8/drivers/gpu/drm/drm_panel.c) and [7.1.2 counterpart](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gregkh/linux/v7.1.2/drivers/gpu/drm/drm_panel.c).
- Generic disable blanks the backlight before the panel disable callback. The latter sends display-off, waits 20 ms, enters sleep and waits 120 ms; unprepare asserts reset and disables supplies. These paths, their return handling and all power/reset delays are unchanged.
- Inherited limits remain: normal brightness read/write callbacks leave LPM cleared on a failed transfer and force it on after success; prepare rollback does not check bulk-disable failure or explicitly assert reset; unprepare ignores regulator-disable status. The DRM core logs a backlight-enable failure after marking the panel enabled and provides no automatic retry/transactional rollback. Thus an enable brightness-write failure can leave the now-dark panel dark until a subsequent successful update or cycle. These are existing error-path limitations, not repaired by the one-value init change. No claim of fault-injection or runtime recovery testing is made.
