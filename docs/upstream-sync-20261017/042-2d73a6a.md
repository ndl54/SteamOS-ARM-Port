The reused policy disables writable wake attributes for PMIC-glink power supplies, MHI, PCI devices and remoteproc DSPs. It enables PMIC power-key and common gpio-keys wake attributes. It does not target RTC or arbitrary other wake sources. No new sysfs patterns or kernel/config/patch changes were added. This is the existing full-path policy now reached on light sleep, not a separately validated per-device wake whitelist.
